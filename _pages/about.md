Hi, I’m **Junteng Liu**. I’m a first-year PhD candidate at the **HKUST NLP Group**. My research focuses on **natural language processing** and **machine learning**, with particular interests in **LLM reasoning**, **reinforcement learning**, **LLM truthfulness and interpretability**, and **hallucination in vision-language models**.
